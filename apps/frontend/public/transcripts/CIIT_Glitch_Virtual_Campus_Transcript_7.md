Moving up to 6F, we arrive at a floor dedicated to learning, creativity, and hands-on innovation.

This floor features four lecture classrooms, providing students with dedicated spaces for lessons, discussions, and academic activities.

One of its main highlights is The Forge: Maker Lab, a dedicated prototyping, fabrication, and makerspace laboratory where students can turn their ideas into tangible projects.

Inside, you'll find electronic workstations, microcontrollers, single-board computers, and equipment for 3D printing and slicing. These tools allow students to experiment, build prototypes, and explore their creativity through practical, hands-on experiences.

Whether it's learning inside the classroom or bringing an idea to life inside the Maker Lab, 6F encourages students to go beyond theory and explore the possibilities of technology and innovation.
