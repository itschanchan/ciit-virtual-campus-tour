Moving up, we arrive at the Upper Ground Floor, an open and welcoming space designed for students to gather, unwind, and enjoy their free time.

This floor features plenty of tables and seating areas, making it a perfect spot to grab a meal, catch up with friends, work on group activities, or simply relax between classes.

Along the sides, you'll also find various stores where students and visitors can grab food, drinks, and other essentials.

Unlike restricted areas of the building, the Upper Ground is open to everyone, making it an accessible and lively space for the entire CIIT community.

Whether you're looking for a place to hang out, enjoy a quick break, or spend time with friends, the Upper Ground is where campus life comes together!
