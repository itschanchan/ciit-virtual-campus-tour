As we continue our tour, we arrive at 9F, a floor that brings together academic spaces and a place for students to take a well-deserved break.

This floor features several lecture rooms where students can attend classes, participate in discussions, and continue their academic activities.

One of its main highlights is the Canteen, a spacious dining area equipped with plenty of seating to accommodate students during their meal breaks.

What makes this space even more special is its beautiful view of the city, giving students a chance to enjoy their meals while taking in the surrounding skyline.

Whether you're grabbing a quick bite between classes, sharing a meal with friends, or simply enjoying the view, 9F offers a perfect place to recharge before heading back to learning.
