Next, we make our way to 4F, a two-level space dedicated to learning, collaboration, and student support.

Here, students can find designated study areas where they can focus on their academic work, as well as meeting rooms for group discussions, meetings, and collaborative activities.

This area also houses the campus clinic, providing students with access to basic medical assistance when needed.

Additionally, 4F is home to the IT Department, which provides technical support, and the Guidance Rooms, where students can seek guidance, advice, and personal support.

Whether you're here to study, work with classmates, or access the support services you need, 4F brings together the essential spaces that help make student life more convenient and comfortable.
