We'll begin our tour at the Lower Ground Floor, which serves as one of the main entry points to Glitch Tower.

As you enter, you'll find the lobby, where security personnel are stationed to assist visitors and help maintain the building's safety.

For students and authorized personnel, access is managed through ID turnstiles. Simply scan your CIIT ID to gain entry and proceed into the building.

This floor also houses the building's elevators, which serve as the main gateway to the other floors of Glitch Tower, making it easy to navigate and explore the entire campus.
