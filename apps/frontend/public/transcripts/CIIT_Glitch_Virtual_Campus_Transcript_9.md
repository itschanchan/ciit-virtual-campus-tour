Moving up to 8F, we find another floor dedicated to both digital learning and creative development.

This floor features classrooms and PC laboratories equipped to support students in their computer-based academic activities and projects.

One of its notable highlights is the specialized Pen Labs, designed particularly for students pursuing creative programs such as BS Multimedia Arts.

These facilities provide students with access to digital drawing tools and pen-enabled equipment, allowing them to explore digital illustration, graphic design, and other forms of digital art.

By combining traditional classroom learning with specialized digital facilities, 8F gives aspiring artists and creators the tools they need to develop their skills and bring their creative visions to life.
