Moving on to 7F, we arrive at another floor dedicated to learning and creative development.

This floor features four classrooms where students can participate in lectures, discussions, and various academic activities.

It is also home to two specialized facilities: the Mac Lab and the 3D Lab.

The Mac Lab provides students with access to Mac computers for digital work, creative projects, and other computer-based activities. Meanwhile, the 3D Lab offers a dedicated space for students to explore 3D-related work and develop their skills in three-dimensional design and creation.

Together, these facilities provide students with spaces and tools to explore their creativity, develop technical skills, and bring their ideas to life.
